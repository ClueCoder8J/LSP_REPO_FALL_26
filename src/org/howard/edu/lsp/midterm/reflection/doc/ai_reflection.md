Benefits:
AI has helped in explaining certain concepts that may have been more difficult for me to grasp otherwise.
For example, I used AI to help explain how to break classes down into subclasses

Challenges:
Quite frequently I have had to tell AI that it was incorrect in its suggestion or implementation of something.
One specific example was when I was using a recently updated library and it gave me code that would work in old versions.
I had to repeatedly tell it about the update, even providing evidence before it believed and helped adjust the code.

Moving Forward:
I intend to continue using AI as minimally as possible, mostly for explaining concepts and trying to rely on my own knowledge.
